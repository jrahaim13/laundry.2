About Canam

Canam Steel Corporation (CSC) designs and manufactures open-web steel joists and steel deck, with engineering and project support for commercial, industrial, institutional, and multi-residential work.

Query note: “Canal Steel” most often maps to Canam Steel (cscsteelusa.com / Canam Group). There is also an obscure NY filing for Canal Steel, Inc., which is not a major joist/deck player.

Primary U.S. competitors

These firms routinely show up on the same joist and deck packages as Canam.

Largest · Nucor

Vulcraft

Nucor’s joist and deck division—North America’s scale leader for open-web joists, joist girders, steel deck, and grating. The competitor Canam meets most often on national bids.

#2 after CMC exit · Steel Dynamics

New Millennium Building Systems

Steel Dynamics division making joists, metal deck, and related engineered products. Expanded by acquiring CMC’s joist and deck plants, putting it firmly in the top tier with Vulcraft and Canam.

Regional national · EBSCO

Valley Joist + Deck

Long-running joist and deck manufacturer (EBSCO Industries). Competes on engineered product systems where Canam also bids fabricators and GCs.

Historical peer · CMC

CMC Joists & Deck

Formerly a major U.S. producer. CMC exited joists and deck; New Millennium absorbed key plants. Still cited in older competitive maps alongside Canam and Vulcraft.

SJI / SDI certified

Altra

Certified open-web joist manufacturer competing for specification-driven work in the same SJI/SDI ecosystem as Canam.

Adjacent fabricators

Structural steel peers

On broader structural packages, Canam-adjacent names include Canatal Industries, Cives Steel, High Steel Structures, and Schuff / Quincy Joist lineage—more fabricator overlap than pure joist/deck rivals.

Canada & regional rivals

Outside the big three U.S. joist/deck names, Canam Group has faced regional specialists.

  • Vicwest — Canadian building products competitor historically cited vs. Canam.
  • Samuel / Omega Joists — Western Canada joist competition.
  • Delta Joists — Quebec regional joist competitor.
  • MBS Steel — Ontario-level competition.
  • ASC Steel Deck / Epic Metals — Regional deck alternatives on some packages.

How the field stacks up

For joists and deck, think Vulcraft first, then New Millennium and Canam as the core national set, with Valley Joist and certified regionals filling local packages. Specifiers often compare EPDs and SJI/SDI credentials as much as price.
